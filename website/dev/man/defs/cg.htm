Calendar Description - Scheme cg

This scheme presents the traditional Chinese lunisolar calendar with the Gregorian calendar’s year number. The underlying structure remains fully Chinese — lunar months, leap months, solar terms, and sexagenary cycle — but the year count is taken directly from the Gregorian civil calendar. This hybrid reflects the modern coexistence of two parallel systems: the lunisolar calendar for cultural and seasonal purposes, and the Gregorian calendar for civil, administrative, and international use.

See Wikipedia entry.

Use Case

This form has been in practical use since 1912, when the Republic of China adopted the Gregorian calendar as its official civil calendar. From that point onward, lunisolar dates were routinely expressed within the Gregorian year, especially in newspapers, almanacs, and public communication. Expressions such as “Chinese New Year 2024” or “Lunar 8th Month, 15th Day (2026)” became standard. Although never an official reform of the Chinese calendar itself, this pairing is now the dominant modern framing for Chinese festival dates across mainland China, Taiwan, Hong Kong, and overseas Chinese communities.

Definition

Record - Default Order
yearmonthlmonthday cycle segyear stem branch element polarity clmonth

The Record consists of four required Fields named year, month, lmonth, and day. There are twelve regular months in a year numbered 1 to 12. The lmonth field is set to 1 for these months. In a leap year, one of the months will be repeated with the lmonth field set to 2. Each month starts on day 1, the day of the new moon, and will be 29 or 30 days long.

The additional cycle field counts the number of 60 year sexagenary cycles since the year -2636 Gregorian. The additional segyear field is the year within the sexagenary cycle.

The additional stem and branch fields are the year stem and branch of the sexagenary cycle. The additional element and polarity fields are the element and polarity of the year stem.

The calculated field clmonth is a copy of the lmonth field when one of the months is a leap month pair and an invalid or null value otherwise. It is used to display the leap month status in formats where it is omitted if not required.

Formats

The lmonth field only needs to be displayed when there are a leap month pair, for other months it is redundant. The calculated clmonth field is a copy of the lmonth when one of a leap month pair and a null value otherwise.

The formats that show the sexagenary year and cycle number and not the epoch year are available in the Chinese scheme "c".

Script

Script - Module hics:chinese
scheme cg { name "Chinese with Gregorian Year"; base chinese "year:+2637" additional; grammar c; };

The scheme "cg" uses the grammar "c" definition defined in c Chinese scheme which is also in the hics:chinese module.
